Letterpress studio, est. 2014

Paper, ink, and the patience to get it right.

We design and hand-print stationery on a century-old Vandercook press — wedding suites, business cards, and small-batch editions, each one proofed and pulled by hand before it leaves the shop.

What we make

Every piece starts as a hand-set or photopolymer plate and ends up debossed into cotton paper. No two runs are identical, and that's the point.

Wedding Suites

Invitations, save-the-dates, and day-of stationery designed around your story, printed in editions as small as twenty-five.

Business Stationery

Letterhead, business cards, and notecards for studios and small firms who want paper that feels as considered as their work.

Custom Editions

Broadsides, prints, and one-off commissions — bring us a phrase, a poem, or an idea, and we'll set it in type.

Made the slow way

Design happens in the studio; printing happens on the press floor next door — one sheet at a time, fed and pulled by hand.
