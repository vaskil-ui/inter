A small contact form that remembers what you told it.

This page is Task 3 of the internship: a contact form with client-side validation, saved straight to your browser's storage, and read back on the submissions page.

  1. 01

    Fill in the form

    Name, email, and a short message — every field is required.

  2. 02

    JavaScript checks it

    Empty fields and malformed emails are caught before anything is saved.

  3. 03

    It's saved to LocalStorage

    No server, no database — the entry lives in your browser until you clear it.

  4. 04

    Read it back

    The submissions page lists everything you've saved, newest first.
